Questions

Before you visit

What’s the difference from Spa Access Only?

Spa Access Only is just the spa: pool, sauna, lounge, drinks, towel and robe. Spa, Lunch & Treatment adds lunch and a 20-minute massage or facial each. Both come as a half day or a full day.

Can I book more treatments?

Yes. Add massages, facials or a Japanese head spa for anyone in your group when you book. Head spa treatments are 10% off with a spa day.

Can we have the spa to ourselves?

Yes. With 6 guests you get 10% off and the spa is yours for your session at no extra cost, on days the spa is free. With 5 or fewer, tick “Make it just for us” when you book; there’s a small charge for each empty place: £25 for a half day, £40 for a full day, or £75 a night on a spa break (50% off within 7 days of your visit). Guests booked only for treatments may still come in.

Can you cater for allergies or vegan diets?

Yes, we cater for all dietary requirements. Tell us when you book.

Can 16 and 17-year-olds come?

Yes, with an adult. Under-16s can come only when the spa is booked just for your group.

What if I need to change my date?

Spa days are non-refundable, but you can move your visit (subject to availability) by emailing us at least 30 days before.
