Cookies Policy

About cookies

Only what the website needs. No tracking.

Last updated 28 November 2026. This policy explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser’s own storage.

The short version

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t set any cookies of our own. That’s why you won’t see a cookie banner. We only use what the website needs to work, and anonymous visitor counts that don’t identify you.

What we use

  • Your booking in progress (session storage, strictly necessary): remembers your choices while you book, so nothing is lost if you go back a step or refresh the page. It is deleted when you close the tab or finish booking.
  • Website text (local storage, strictly necessary): keeps a copy of the site’s latest wording so pages load quickly. It contains no personal information.
  • Visitor counts (GoatCounter, no cookies): counts page views anonymously. It doesn’t use cookies or store anything on your device, and doesn’t identify you or follow you to other websites.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): your browser downloads our fonts from Google, which sees your device’s internet address as part of the request. No cookies are set.

Other websites

Links to Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps, what3words and other businesses take you to their websites, which have their own cookies and privacy policies. Payment pages from Stripe or Klarna may use cookies to keep payments secure and prevent fraud.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings. If you block site storage, the website will still work, but your booking choices won’t be remembered if you refresh the page.

Changes and questions

If we ever start using cookies that aren’t strictly necessary, we’ll update this policy and ask for your consent first. Any questions? send us a message. See our Privacy Policy for how we look after your information.
