Spa Break · from £150 per person

Stay a night, or a few

Sunday to Thursday nights, 1 to 5 nights, for 1 to 16 guests. Spa time every day, lunch included and the kitchen for your dinner.

What’s included

Your time away

  • A bedroom for every 2 guests, with premium toiletries and linens
  • The spa from 2pm on the day you arrive until 6pm on your last evening
  • Lunch every day you’re with us
  • The guest kitchen and BBQ for your evening meal
  • A 20-minute aromatherapy massage or facial each
  • Hot and cold drinks, robe and towel
  • 1 night£150 per person
  • 2 nights£250 per person
  • 3 nights£350 per person
  • 4 nights£450 per person
  • 5 nights£550 per person

Prices are per person, based on 2 sharing a room. A guest with a room to themselves adds £50 a night (not charged for 6 or more guests or “just for us”, as every bedroom is yours). Continental breakfast is £10 per person per morning. Groups of 6 or more get 10% off and the spa to themselves on dates it’s free.

Just for your group

  • 6 or more guests: 10% off, and the spa is yours during your spa times with no other guests staying those nights, at no extra cost, on dates the spa is free.
  • 5 or fewer: tick “Make it just for us” when you book. There’s a small charge for each empty place: £25 for a half day, £40 for a full day, or £75 a night on a spa break (50% off within 7 days of your visit).
  • Guests booked only for treatments may still come in for their treatment.
