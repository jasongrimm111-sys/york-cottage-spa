Good to know

Answers to the questions guests ask most. Can’t find yours? Ask our assistant for an instant answer.

Spa days

Can I book more treatments as well as those included?

Yes. Add them when you book, or see the treatment menu. We recommend booking extra treatments ahead of your visit.

Can we bring our own food and drink?

Yes, you’re welcome to bring snacks and drinks. We’re happy to supply bowls, cutlery and glassware.

How many people will be in the spa with us?

No more than 6 guests share the spa at once. Book 6 and you get 10% off, with the spa yours for your session at no extra cost on days the spa is free. With fewer, tick “Make it just for us” when you book: there’s a small charge for each empty place: £25 for a half day, £40 for a full day, or £75 a night on a spa break, and it’s 50% off within 7 days of your visit. Guests booked only for treatments may still come in.

Can we have our afternoon tea or grazing board before or after the spa?

Yes, we recommend it to make the most of your spa time. Let us know if you’d like to arrive early, and you’re welcome to enjoy the gardens and lounges afterwards.

Are children allowed in the spa?

16 and 17-year-olds are welcome on spa days with an adult. Under-16s can use the spa only when it’s booked just for your group (a spa day or spa break of 6, or “Make it just for us”) or on a whole-house booking. Under-18s must be with an adult at all times.

Can you cater for nut allergies or food intolerances?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we’ll take care of it.

Can we have a vegan or veggie afternoon tea or grazing board?

Yes, we cater for all dietary requirements. You’ll be asked when you book.

Do you have a Blue Light discount?

Blue Light Card holders get their included treatments upgraded to 30 minutes.

Spa Breaks

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 13:00 when you arrive Monday to Thursday, and from 14:00 on Sundays. Check-out is 11:00, and you’re welcome to enjoy the house and gardens afterwards.

What time will our spa access be?

From 14:00 to 18:00 on the day you arrive (18:00 to 22:30 on Sundays), and 09:30 to 18:00 on the days in between. Treatments can be any time during your stay (from 17:30 on Sundays).

What’s available for breakfast?

Continental breakfast (£10 per person) needs to be pre-ordered. Add it when you book.

Can we order a takeaway?

Yes. Indian, Chinese, Italian and Turkish takeaways deliver to us; menus are in the house guide.

Are there restaurants nearby?

Yes, a short drive away, from fine dining to pub food. See our local recommendations.

Are there cooking facilities?

Yes, a communal guest kitchen with an oven and cooking equipment.

Can we hire a private chef?

Yes. Get in touch and we’ll put you in touch with a recommended chef.

Can you store our luggage?

Yes, before or after check-in.

General

Is there free parking?

Yes, plenty.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free throughout the property.

Are treatments safe during pregnancy?

Yes. Our therapists are trained in pregnancy massage. Tell us you’re pregnant at your consultation.

Do you have an electric car charger?

We have one charger, first come first served.

Can I get a VAT receipt?

Yes. Send us a message and we’ll arrange it.

Can I bring my dog?

Pets aren’t allowed, but working animals are welcome.

Whole House

Do we have the spa to ourselves?

Yes. We won’t book any other guests into the spa during your stay. On check-out day the spa is available until 10:30 so it can be cleaned for the next guests.

How does the damage deposit work?

You’ll get a payment link within 48 hours of check-in to pre-authorise £500. After our property check, it’s released within 48 hours of check-out, minus any deductions.

Can we book for 1 night?

Whole-house stays include Friday and Saturday night, and you can add nights either side. From Sunday to Thursday, book a Spa Break for 1 to 16 guests instead.

Do you include firewood and charcoal?

We include one basket of firewood for the open fire and fire pit. A garage 3 miles away sells more, plus charcoal for the BBQ.

Gift Vouchers

How do I redeem my gift voucher?

Enter the code on your voucher in the gift voucher box when you pay, and bring the voucher with you.

Do gift vouchers expire?

Yes, they must be used within 12 months of issue.
