Privacy Policy

Your privacy

How we look after your information.

Last updated 28 November 2026. This policy explains what personal information York Cottage Spa collects when you use this website, book with us, buy a gift voucher or get in touch, why we need it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the rights you have. It is written to meet the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

York Cottage Spa Ltd (“we”, “us”) of York Cottage, Easton Lane, Bozeat, Northamptonshire NN29 7NN is the controller of your personal information. If you have any question about this policy or your information, send us a message, call 01933 580 100 or write to us at the address above, marked “Data protection”.

What we collect

  • Booking details: the lead guest’s name, email address and mobile number; the name of each guest; what each guest has chosen (treatments, food, rooms); the dates and times of your visit; and any notes you give us.
  • Health information: the answers on your consultation form before treatments or spa use, such as medical conditions, medicines, allergies and pregnancy. This is “special category” information, so we only collect it with your explicit consent, and only to keep you safe.
  • Payment details: payments are taken by our payment providers (Stripe and Klarna). We see the amount, the method and the last four digits of a card, but never your full card details.
  • Gift vouchers: the buyer’s name and email, and the recipient’s name and any message.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form, the chat assistant, the waiting list, “manage my booking” requests and emails, with our replies.
  • Marketing choices: whether you have agreed to hear about news and offers, and when.
  • How the site is used: anonymous visitor counts (pages viewed, the website you came from, browser and country). These don’t identify you and use no cookies. See our Cookies Policy.

If you book for other people, please make sure they are happy for you to give us their names and let them know about this policy. Each guest fills in their own consultation form.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To take and manage your booking (contract): to confirm it, plan treatment times and rooms, take payment, send confirmations, reminders and changes, and look after you on the day.
  • To give treatments safely (explicit consent, and our duty of care): using your consultation form so our therapists know what is safe for you.
  • To keep proper accounts (legal obligation): records of bookings, payments and refunds for HMRC.
  • To answer your questions (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract): replying to messages, chat questions and waiting-list requests.
  • Text messages (consent): only if you tick “Text me as well”. Booking emails always go out because they are part of your booking.
  • News and offers (consent): only if you agree. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Running and protecting the website (legitimate interests): keeping it secure, preventing fraud and misuse, and improving it using anonymous visitor counts.

We don’t make decisions about you using only automated means, and we never sell or rent your information or share it for other businesses’ marketing.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the spa, and only what they need. They act on our instructions and must keep your information secure:

  • Grimmitt Games, who build and manage this website and our booking system.
  • GitHub (hosting of the website and secure storage of booking records) and Cloudflare (the small server that handles forms and bookings).
  • Stripe and Klarna (payments). They may also use your details to prevent fraud and meet their own legal duties, as explained in their privacy policies.
  • Resend (sending emails) and Twilio (sending text messages, if you choose them).
  • Anthropic (the AI behind our chat assistant, when it answers a question). Please don’t put health or payment details into the chat.
  • Google (website fonts, maps and our Google reviews) and GoatCounter (anonymous visitor counts).

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or with professional advisers and insurers.

Information stored outside the UK

Some of these providers store or process information in the United States or the European Economic Area. Where that happens, it is protected by UK adequacy rules (including the UK–US Data Bridge) or the UK’s approved contract clauses, so your information keeps the same level of protection as in the UK.

How long we keep it

  • Bookings and payments: 6 years after your visit, for our accounts.
  • Consultation forms: 7 years after your last treatment, in line with our insurers’ guidance, then securely deleted.
  • Messages and chat: 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Waiting list: until the date you asked about has passed.
  • Gift vouchers: 6 years after the voucher expires or is used.
  • Marketing: until you unsubscribe. We then keep your email address on a “do not contact” list so we don’t email you again.

How we keep it safe

Booking records are kept in private, access-controlled storage. Consultation forms are encrypted on your device before they are sent and can only be opened by our team. Staff only see what they need for their job, and our team signs in with individual accounts.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, where we don’t need to keep it;
  • ask us to restrict or stop using it, or object to how we use it;
  • ask for your information in a format you can give to someone else;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for example to marketing or texts), without affecting anything we did before.

To use any of these rights, send us a message or call 01933 580 100. It’s free, and we’ll reply within one month. We may need to check your identity first.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

Our services are booked by adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children, except the names (and, where a parent or guardian completes one, a consultation form) of under-18s booked into a visit by an adult.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page if anything changes, and show the date at the top. If a change affects you significantly, we’ll let you know by email.
