Booking terms

Please read these before you book.

About these terms

A contract with York Cottage Spa Ltd begins when we receive payment and send a booking confirmation showing your dates. It applies to you and everyone in your party, so please make sure they accept these terms and the documents they include. Not sharing relevant information, or not following these terms, may mean your booking is cancelled.

1. Spa Days and Spa Breaks

Reservations. All online spa day and Spa Break bookings are paid in full. Exclusive use (“Make it just for us”) for fewer than 6 guests has a small charge for each empty place: £25 for a half day, £40 for a full day, or £75 a night on a spa break, 50% off within 7 days of your visit. There’s no refund for leaving early unless caused by significant negligence by us or our staff. Promotional rates can’t be combined with other offers. Included treatments are 20 minutes unless stated otherwise.

Spa day cancellation. Spa days are non-refundable. You can reschedule, subject to availability, by emailing us at least 30 days before your visit.

Spa break cancellation. Overnight spa breaks are refundable with at least 30 days’ notice. With less notice, a 100% cancellation fee applies.

2. Whole House bookings

Payment. 50% is due when you book and the balance 6 weeks before your stay. Bookings made within 6 weeks are paid in full.

Cancellation. With 6 months’ notice or more, your dates can be moved to another available date. With 6 weeks’ notice or more, the deposit is kept as a cancellation fee. With less than 6 weeks’ notice, the full payment is kept. Leaving early incurs the full cost of the remaining nights. We strongly recommend travel insurance.

Occupancy and age. No parties or events. A maximum of 16 people may use the accommodation at any time. We can’t accept bookings from anyone under 18, and we may refuse any booking.

Check-in and check-out. Stays normally start at 16:00 unless agreed otherwise. Please leave by 11:00 on departure day; cleaners may arrive from 09:00.

Damage deposit. A £500 card pre-authorisation is needed before check-in. We send a payment link 48 hours before arrival, check the property at check-out, and report any damage within 48 hours. You’re responsible for any loss or damage caused by your party.

House rules. No pets (working animals excepted), smoking or vaping indoors, or candles in the house; any of these ends the booking without refund. Don’t move furniture between rooms, keep noise low between 22:00 and 07:00, use designated parking, and lock up when you go out. The spa pool is available 08:00 to 22:00; please shower first and keep glass away from the pool. Use the BBQ only on hard ground. Children must be supervised at all times. Please report any problem while you’re still with us; complaints made after leaving can’t be considered.

Other. We may enter for essential maintenance or inspection. We aren’t liable for temporary equipment faults or utility outages, or for lost belongings. The property may not be sublet.

3. General conditions

Gift vouchers. Please bring your voucher with you. Vouchers used to pay are void if you don’t arrive. They are non-refundable, have no cash value and must be used within 12 months.

Breakdowns. We’ll fix mechanical or electrical faults as quickly as we can. Refunds are only given if a fault is due to our negligence.

Valuables. We can’t accept responsibility for money or valuables, so please insure them yourself.

Children and pets. Pets aren’t allowed, except working animals. Under-18s must be supervised by an adult at all times, especially around the spa pool. Under-16s can use the spa only when it’s booked just for your group (a spa day or spa break of 6, or “Make it just for us”) or on a whole-house booking. Under-18s aren’t permitted on spa breaks.

Health. We aren’t a medical centre. Please check with your doctor if you’re unsure about treatments, the hot tub or the sauna, tell us if you feel unwell, and complete a consultation form before any treatment or spa access.

Photos. Photos on this website are for illustration and may not show the exact space you’ll use.

4. Documents and acceptance

These terms include the House Rules in our House Welcome Guide and our Privacy Policy. By booking or using our services you accept these terms, which may change from time to time.
