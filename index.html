Good to know

Before you arrive

A few things guests often ask. For anything else, ask our assistant for an instant answer, or call 01933 580 100.

Email the team
What's included in a spa day?

Spa Access Only is the spa pool, sauna and lounge with hot and cold drinks, towel and robe. Spa, Lunch & Treatment adds lunch (afternoon tea or a grazing board) and a 20-minute aromatherapy massage or facial each. Both come as a half day or a full day.

Can I change my booking?

Spa days are non-refundable, but can be rescheduled (subject to availability) if you email us at least 30 days before your visit. Spa breaks can be refunded with at least 30 days' notice.

Is there an age limit?

Guests must be 18 or over to book. Under-16s can use the spa only when it's booked just for your group (a spa day or spa break of 6, or “Make it just for us”) or on a whole-house booking, and under-18s can't join spa breaks.

What are the session and check-in times?

Spa days run as a half day, 09:30–13:30 or 13:00–17:00, or a full day, 09:30–17:00. On spa breaks, check-in is from 13:00 with the spa 14:00–18:00 on arrival (Monday to Thursday) and 09:30–18:00 on the days in between, or from 14:00 with the spa 18:00–22:30 on Sundays. Whole-house stays check in from 16:00. Check-out is 11:00.

How much is the whole house?

From £1,840 per night for exclusive use of the house and spa, for up to 16 guests. Stays include Friday and Saturday night, and you can add nights either side. Rates can vary by date.

Can I bring my dog?

We love them, but we can only welcome working animals at York Cottage.

How do gift vouchers work?

Vouchers are non-refundable and have no cash value. Please bring yours with you on arrival.
