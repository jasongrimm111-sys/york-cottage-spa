Good to know

Before you arrive

A few things guests often ask. Anything else, just give us a call or message us on WhatsApp.

What's included in a day spa?

A 20-minute aromatherapy massage or facial, hot and cold refreshments, afternoon tea or a grazing board, and full use of the spa pool, sauna and lounge. Towels and robes are provided.

Can I change my booking?

Spa days are non-refundable, but can be rescheduled (subject to availability) if you email us at least 30 days before your visit. Spa breaks can be refunded with at least 30 days' notice.

Is there an age limit?

Guests must be 18 or over to book. Under-16s can use the spa only during a Private Spa session or a whole-house booking, and under-18s can't join spa breaks.

What time is check-in for stays?

Check-in is normally from 4pm and check-out is by 11am.

Can I bring my dog?

We love them, but we can only welcome working animals at York Cottage.

How do gift vouchers work?

Vouchers are non-refundable and have no cash value. Please bring yours with you on arrival.
